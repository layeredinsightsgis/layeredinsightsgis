What changes the price

  • How true to scale your plans are
  • Floor size
  • How much goes on the map: rooms only, or shut-offs, panels and contacts too
  • Whether we need to measure on site

Not just drawings. Every floor comes back placed on the map, with rooms numbered and measured and the things people need to find, ready to use in your GIS, our portal or a simple web map.

Larger portfolios (10+ floors) are quoted with a schedule that fits, so you know when each building will be done. We're a small practice, and we'll tell you honestly how long it will take.

Every floor plan includes one round of corrections. Most single floors are delivered in one to two weeks, depending on our schedule. Plans drawn from "not to scale" sources are good for finding rooms and equipment, not for construction or measured layout.

Questions

About the prices.

Why do prices start "from"?
Floors vary in size and in how many rooms and features they have. "From" is the price for a typical small floor. You get a firm price before any work starts.
What makes a job cost more?
Large or very detailed floors, plans that disagree with each other, rush deadlines, and on-site work. We'll tell you up front if any of these apply.
What do I get at the end?
Your data in standard GIS formats you own, plus a PDF of each floor. If you use the portal, your buildings are already loaded.
Do you make CAD drawings?
No. We're GIS specialists, not CAD drafters. We turn your CAD, PDFs and paper plans into GIS, and we'll help you sort out the drawing problems we find along the way, like rooms that don't close or room numbers that don't match. If you need new CAD drawings made, we'll point you to a drafting firm.
Is my project kept confidential?
Yes. We work under a non-disclosure agreement, signed before you share any files. We don't discuss your project, name you as a client, or show your buildings to anyone outside your organization without your written permission.
How does payment work?
Hourly consulting and small fixed-price jobs are invoiced on delivery. Larger projects start with a deposit, set by the scope of work, with the balance on delivery. The terms are written into every quote.
Do you travel?
Most work is done remotely from the plans you send. On-site measurement is quoted separately, including travel.
