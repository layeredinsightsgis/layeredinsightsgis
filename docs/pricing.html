Questions

About the prices.

Why do prices start "from"?
Floors vary in size and in how many rooms and features they have. "From" is the price for a typical small floor. You get a firm price before any work starts.
What makes a job cost more?
Large or very detailed floors, plans that disagree with each other, rush deadlines, and on-site work. We'll tell you up front if any of these apply.
What do I get at the end?
Your data in standard GIS formats you own, plus a PDF of each floor. If you use the portal, your buildings are already loaded.
Do you make CAD drawings?
No. We're GIS specialists, not CAD drafters. We turn your CAD, PDFs and paper plans into GIS, and we'll help you sort out the drawing problems we find along the way, like rooms that don't close or room numbers that don't match. If you need new CAD drawings made, we'll point you to a drafting firm.
Do you travel?
Most work is done remotely from the plans you send. On-site measurement is quoted separately, including travel.
