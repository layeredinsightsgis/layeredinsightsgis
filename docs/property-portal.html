Property portal · early access

Every building's map and memory, in one place.

Where's the water shut-off? Who services the fire panel? Is that after-hours number still good? The answers usually live in one person's head or on a marked-up site plan. Our portal puts them on a map of each of your buildings, alongside tenants, vendors and work orders, and tells you what's missing before someone needs it.

Request a demo How it works

What it keeps track of

The building, and everything a manager needs to know about it.

Each building is modeled floor by floor: suites, rooms, the core, and the things a responder or a new manager needs to find first.

Building map

Floors, suites and rooms on a real plan, with a stacking view of who is where and what is vacant.

Tenants and contacts

Primary, after-hours and billing contacts for every tenant, with the date each one was last confirmed.

Vendors and work orders

Who services what, how to reach them, and a simple job log tied to the unit, floor or room it happened in.

Safety and systems

Shut-offs, fire alarm panel, FDC, generator, roof access and assembly points, with a check for what's missing or out of date.

Why it matters

The knowledge that leaves with people stays with the building.

  • Buildings change hands. The knowledge shouldn't.Ownership and management turn over every few years, and knowing where the water shut-off is tends to be the first thing lost. Here it stays with the building, ready to hand to the next manager.
  • No GIS person needed.Staff add a shut-off or a fire panel by picking what it is and clicking the map. Floors, rooms and coordinates are handled for them.
  • Sensitive locations stay internal.Cameras, lockboxes and network rooms can be marked manager-only and are left off tenant plans and anything shared.
  • Works alongside what you already use.Accounting and work order systems stay where they are. The portal holds what those systems don't: the building itself.

How it's built

Proven platforms, and your data stays yours.

Built on Esri's ArcGIS, the mapping platform cities, counties, universities and utilities use for their own facilities, with a secure portal in front of it. Each login only ever sees its own buildings, and that is enforced on the server, not just hidden on the screen.

Building data is kept in an industry-standard geodatabase. It can be exported and handed over at any time, for example when a building is sold. A detailed security overview is available on request.
